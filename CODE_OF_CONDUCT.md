# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment of any
kind is not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to **saisanirose@gmail.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially, and you will get a reply within 7 days. If your report concerns the person who
maintains this project, say so and we will find an uninvolved party to review it.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face temporary or permanent
consequences, as determined by the project's other maintainers. The
[full enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe the escalation we follow.
